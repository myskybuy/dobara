About Dobara
Giving good things a second life — close to home.

“Dobara” means “again”. We’re building the most trusted way for people in India to buy and sell pre-loved things with their neighbours — without the haggling anxiety, fake listings or payment scares that usually come with it.

Why we exist
Every home has things worth passing on

A camera used on two trips, a study table outgrown, a bicycle replaced by a scooter. These things still have years of use left, but selling them has meant awkward calls, strangers asking for advance payments and meet-ups that don’t feel safe.

Dobara fixes the parts that make second-hand risky. Payments are held until the buyer has the item, every handover is confirmed with a one-time code, and sellers show what they’ve verified — so both sides can focus on a fair deal.
